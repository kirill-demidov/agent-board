agentboard

One board for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and opencode.

One card is one conversation. Drag cards where you want them, group them by project, and see at a glance who is working, who finished, and who is waiting for your reply.

Waiting for you

When an agent stops to ask for approval, its card turns amber and the board counts it in the header. No more finding out forty minutes later.

Reply from the card

Answer an agent without opening a terminal. Open the live terminal when you actually want it.

Nothing is lost

Cards survive reboots: a live agent becomes paused and resumes with one click. Removing a card never deletes the conversation history.

Local by design

The board reads the CLIs' own logs and talks to tmux on your machine. No account, no telemetry, nothing leaves the laptop.

Three skins

Terminal phosphor, macOS glass, Soviet retro console — each with a night mode.
